# Project Roadmap

The vision for **AI Handwritten Notes Generator** is to build the premier open-source exam revision engine for university students worldwide.

---

## Phase 1: Core Foundation (Completed - v1.0.0)
- [x] AI Answer Synthesizer for 10-Mark and 5-Mark university formats.
- [x] Multi-font realistic handwriting simulation (6 Google web fonts).
- [x] Notebook paper ruling with margins, binder rings, and graph paper textures.
- [x] Multi-color ink simulation (Royal Blue, Black Gel, Crimson Red, Emerald Highlighter).
- [x] Hand-drawn style flowcharts and comparison tables.
- [x] PyMuPDF study material and syllabus PDF extractor.
- [x] High-resolution ReportLab vector A4 PDF export.
- [x] Pillow & html2canvas PNG export.
- [x] Native Telugu + English bilingual explanations.
- [x] Full-featured Offline Demo Mode.
- [x] SQLite database with SQLAlchemy ORM.

---

## Phase 2: Expanded Language & Mathematical Intelligence (Target: v1.1.0)
- [ ] **LaTeX Formula Handwriting:** Render complex mathematical proofs, calculus integrals, and chemical formulas into clean handwritten equivalents.
- [ ] **Additional Indian Regional Languages:** Support bilingual explanations in Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, and Marathi.
- [ ] **Voice-to-Notes:** Dictate lecture notes directly from audio recordings into handwritten study sheets.

---

## Phase 3: Collaborative Features & Cloud Sync (Target: v1.2.0)
- [ ] **Study Group Collaboration:** Share editable study sheets with classmates via secure shareable links.
- [ ] **Anki & Flashcard Export:** One-click conversion of handwritten notes into digital flashcards.
- [ ] **OCR Handwriting Customization:** Allow students to upload a photo of their own handwriting sample to synthesize a custom personalized font profile.
